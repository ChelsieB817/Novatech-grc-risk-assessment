NovaTech Solutions – Cybersecurity Risk Assessment Summary

## 1. Executive Summary

This document summarizes a simulated cybersecurity risk assessment conducted for NovaTech Solutions, a fictional SaaS company based in Dallas, Texas.

The purpose of this assessment is to identify potential cybersecurity risks affecting company assets, evaluate their likelihood and impact, and recommend security measures to reduce organizational risk.

The assessment focuses on protecting customer information, employee records, company systems, cloud infrastructure, and business operations.

## 2. Assessment Scope

The assessment reviewed 10 company assets and 10 associated cybersecurity risk scenarios.

The assets included:

- AWS cloud servers
- Customer database
- Employee laptops
- User accounts
- GitHub repository
- Microsoft 365
- APIs
- Backup systems
- Customer support portal
- Employee records

## 3. Risk Assessment Approach

A qualitative risk assessment methodology was used to evaluate each identified risk.

Each risk was assigned:

- A likelihood rating from 1 to 5.
- An impact rating from 1 to 5.
- An inherent risk score calculated by multiplying likelihood by impact.

Risk levels were classified using the following project-defined thresholds:

| Risk Score | Risk Level |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | Low |
| 5–9 | Medium |
| 10–16 | High |
| 17–25 | Critical |

## 4. Key Risk Findings

The assessment identified several cybersecurity concerns that could affect NovaTech Solutions.

Major areas of concern include:

- Unauthorized access to customer information.
- Compromised employee accounts and credentials.
- Malware infections affecting employee devices.
- Cloud security misconfigurations.
- Exposure of company source code.
- Business email compromise.
- Insecure API endpoints.
- Ransomware affecting backup systems.
- Unauthorized access to customer support information.
- Exposure of sensitive employee records.

These findings demonstrate the importance of access management, endpoint protection, cloud security, data protection, and ongoing monitoring.

## 5. Recommended Risk Priorities

The following risks were identified as critical priorities in the project risk register:

- R-001: Unauthorized Customer Database Access.
- R-004: Compromised Employee Accounts.

The remaining risks should also receive attention through the recommended mitigation strategies documented in the risk mitigation plan.

## 6. Recommended Security Improvements

NovaTech Solutions should consider implementing the following security improvements:

1. Require multi-factor authentication for company accounts.
2. Implement role-based access controls.
3. Encrypt sensitive company and customer information.
4. Establish regular vulnerability and configuration reviews.
5. Maintain endpoint protection and software patching.
6. Improve cloud security monitoring.
7. Protect source code repositories.
8. Maintain secure and tested backups.
9. Conduct employee cybersecurity awareness training.
10. Perform periodic access reviews.

## 7. Risk Treatment and Residual Risk

Risk treatment recommendations are documented in the accompanying risk mitigation plan.

The Excel risk register also includes proposed residual risk ratings to illustrate how risk levels may change after recommended controls are implemented.

These residual ratings are estimates and have not been validated through actual security control implementation or testing.

## 8. Limitations

This assessment is a simulated portfolio exercise based on a fictional company.

The assessment was not conducted using actual company systems, vulnerability scans, employee interviews, penetration testing, or incident records.

The findings and recommendations are illustrative and should not be interpreted as the results of an actual security audit.

## 9. Conclusion

This project demonstrates a structured approach to identifying, documenting, evaluating, and treating cybersecurity risks.

By maintaining an asset inventory, developing a risk register, applying a consistent scoring methodology, and recommending security controls, NovaTech Solutions can establish a foundation for managing cybersecurity risks.

The assessment highlights the importance of continuous risk monitoring, access management, security awareness, and ongoing improvement.
