NovaTech Solutions – Risk Assessment & Scoring Methodology
1. Purpose
The purpose of this document is to establish a consistent methodology for evaluating and prioritizing cybersecurity risks identified in the NovaTech Solutions risk register.

Each risk will be evaluated based on its likelihood of occurring and its potential business impact.

2. Risk Assessment Approach
The assessment uses a qualitative 5-point scoring system.

Two factors are evaluated:

Likelihood: The probability that a risk event could occur.

Impact: The potential consequences to the organization if the risk event occurs.

3. Likelihood Scoring
Score	Rating	Description
1	Rare	Unlikely to occur
2	Unlikely	Could occur, but not expected
3	Possible	May occur under certain circumstances
4	Likely	Expected to occur in many circumstances
5	Almost Certain	Expected to occur frequently
4. Impact Scoring
Score	Rating	Description
1	Insignificant	Minimal business disruption
2	Minor	Limited operational impact
3	Moderate	Noticeable disruption or financial impact
4	Major	Significant business disruption or data exposure
5	Severe	Critical business impact, major data breach, or prolonged service disruption
5. Risk Score Calculation
Risk scores are calculated by multiplying likelihood by impact.

Formula:

Risk Score = Likelihood x Impact

Example:

Likelihood = 4

Impact = 5

Risk Score = 4 x 5 = 20

The resulting score is used to determine the risk classification.

6. Risk Classification
Score Range	Risk Level
1–4	Low
5–9	Medium
10–16	High
17–25	Critical
These thresholds are project-defined and are used consistently throughout this simulated assessment.

7. Risk Response Strategies
The organization may use the following risk response strategies:

Mitigate: Implement security controls to reduce likelihood or impact.

Avoid: Eliminate the activity creating the risk.

Transfer: Shift certain financial consequences to another party, such as through insurance or contractual arrangements.

Accept: Formally acknowledge and retain the risk when appropriate.

8. Risk Ownership
Each identified risk should have an assigned risk owner responsible for monitoring the risk, coordinating mitigation activities, and reporting changes in risk status.

9. Assessment Limitations
This is an initial qualitative assessment based on hypothetical company conditions.

Risk scores are illustrative estimates and have not been validated through technical testing, interviews, vulnerability scans, or actual incident data.

10. Conclusion
This methodology establishes a consistent approach for evaluating cybersecurity risks at NovaTech Solutions.

The scoring system will be used to prioritize identified risks, recommend mitigation strategies, and support the development of a risk treatment plan.
