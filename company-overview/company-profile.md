NovaTech Solutions – Company Profile
1. Business Overview
NovaTech Solutions is a fictional Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) startup that provides cloud-based project management software to small and medium-sized businesses.

The company relies on cloud infrastructure, employee devices, customer databases, and third-party software to operate its business.

2. Company Details
Company Name: NovaTech Solutions

Industry: Technology / Software

Location: Dallas, Texas

Number of Employees: 10

Business Model: Cloud-based subscription software

Cloud Provider: Amazon Web Services (AWS)

Development Platform: GitHub

Communication Platform: Microsoft 365

3. Business Operations
NovaTech Solutions develops and maintains cloud-based project management software. The company manages customer accounts, processes support requests, maintains software applications, and stores customer information.

4. Information and Data
The company handles the following types of information:

Customer names and email addresses

Customer account credentials

Business and project information

Employee records

Internal business communications

Application source code

5. Assessment Objective
The objective of this risk assessment is to identify, evaluate, and document cybersecurity and operational risks that could negatively affect NovaTech Solutions.

The assessment will evaluate potential threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood, business impact, and recommended mitigation strategies.

6. Assessment Scope
This assessment includes:

Cloud infrastructure

Customer databases

Employee laptops

User accounts and access management

Software development environments

Business communication systems

Backup and recovery systems

Customer support systems

7. Assessment Role
Role: Junior GRC Analyst

Project Type: Simulated Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

8. Assessment Frameworks
This project will use concepts from:

NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)

NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF)

ISO 31000 Risk Management Principles

NovaTech Solutions is a fictional company created for educational and portfolio development purposes. This assessment is a simulated exercise and does not represent an actual company security audit.
