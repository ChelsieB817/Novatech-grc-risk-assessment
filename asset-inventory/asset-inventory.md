3. Data Classification Definitions
Public: Information approved for public distribution.

Internal: Information intended for authorized company personnel.

Confidential: Sensitive business or customer information requiring restricted access.

4. Asset Protection Considerations
NovaTech Solutions should implement appropriate security controls to protect its assets.

Recommended protection measures include:

Multi-factor authentication (MFA).

Role-based access control (RBAC).

Regular software updates and patch management.

Data encryption.

Secure data backups.

Endpoint protection.

Employee cybersecurity awareness training.

Access reviews and monitoring.
